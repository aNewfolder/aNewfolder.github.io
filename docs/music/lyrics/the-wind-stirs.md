# 起风了（The Wind Stirs）

## 第一版（Edition One）

All the way I walk and stop　这一路上走走停停  
Along the trace of the floating teenage　顺着少年漂流的痕迹  
The moment before stepping out　迈出车站的前一刻  
I can’t stop hesitating　竟有些犹豫  
Merely get the homesick feeling　不禁笑这近乡情怯  
Still cannot avoid　仍无可避免  
But sky above the field　而长野的天  
Remains to be so warm　依旧那么暖  
And wind blows back the past　风吹起了从前

When first got to know the world　从前初识这世间  
Forgetting to leave　万般流连  
Watching horizon reflecting in my eyes　看着天边似在眼前  
But willing to cross fire and walk it for once　也甘愿赴汤蹈火去走它一遍  
Now I travel through the world　如今走过这世间  
Forgetting to leave　万般流连  
Witnessing different faces of the ages　翻过岁月不同侧脸  
Not prepared I showed up in your smile　措不及防闯入你的笑颜

I once lost myself in large of the world　我曾难自拔于世界之大  
And dwell in fantasies within it　也沉溺于其中梦话  
Despite the truth, admit the fate, and not afraid of laugh　不得真假 不做挣扎 不惧笑话  
I once turned my youth time all into him　我曾将青春翻涌成她  
And tuned out summer round fingertip　也曾指尖弹出盛夏  
When your heart stirs, just follow the wind　心之所动 且就随缘去吧  
Walk against the light despite the wind and rain　逆着光行走 任风吹雨打

On a short way I walk and stop　短短的路走走停停  
And it looks a lot more distant　也有了几分的距离  
Not knowing what I’m touching, a story or a mood　不知抚摸的是故事 还是段心情  
Maybe merely looking forward to being enemy of time　也许期待的不过是 与时间为敌  
But when we meet again, with sunlight in the dawn, and we smile delightedly　再次看到你 微凉晨光里 笑得很甜蜜

The night wind blows your white hair on the face side　晚风吹起你鬓间的白发  
And soothe the scar left by memory　抚平回忆留下的疤  
In your eyes are light but dark　你的眼中 明暗交杂  
You smile and a flower comes　一笑生花  
The darkness covering your uneven steps　暮色遮住你蹒跚的步伐  
Pictures hidden in the bedside　走进床头藏起的画  
In the image you talked with your head down　画中的你 低着头说话

I still lose myself in large of the world　我仍感叹于世界之大  
And still fill myself with childish love words　也沉醉于儿时情话  
Despite the truth, admit the fate, and not afraid of laugh　不剩真假 不做挣扎 无谓笑话  
At close I returned my youth back to her　我终将青春还给了她  
Along with summer round fingertip　连同指尖弹出的盛夏  
When your heart stirs, just follow the wind　心之所动 就随风去了  
In the name of love, would you promise once more.　以爱之名 你还愿意吗
